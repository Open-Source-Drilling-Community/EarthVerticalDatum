## Earth Gravitational Model 1984 (EGM84)

`VerticalDatumModelFiles/egm84-30.pgm` is the GeographicLib 30-minute EGM84 geoid grid. Its metadata identifies it as “WGS84 EGM84, 30-minute grid,” records a data timestamp of 2009-08-29, and reports cubic interpolation errors of 0.274 m maximum and 0.014 m RMS. Runtime model information exposes the grid identity, interpolation mode, error estimates, and SHA-256 for traceability.

The model data is distributed separately from the project code and remains subject to its source terms and notices.
